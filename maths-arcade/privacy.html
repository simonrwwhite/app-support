Maths Arcade - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Overview

Maths Arcade is an educational maths game for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy, especially the privacy of children who use this app.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple Game Center — Used for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is managed by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. You can opt out by not signing in to Game Center.
  • Google AdMob — Used to display interstitial advertisements. For users identified as under 13, ads are served in child-directed mode (COPPA-compliant) with no personalisation. Ad serving is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove all ads via the in-app purchase.
  • Apple Speech Recognition — An optional feature that allows answering questions by voice. Audio is processed by Apple's on-device or server-based speech recognition and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Microphone access requires your explicit permission and can be revoked at any time in Settings.

Children's Privacy

Maths Arcade is designed for children. The app:

  • Does not collect personal information from children
  • Defaults to child-directed ad treatment (COPPA-compliant, non-personalised ads)
  • Does not include social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • Does not require an account to play

In-App Purchases

The app offers a single non-consumable in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not process or store any payment information.

Data Storage

Game progress, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This data is not accessible to us and is removed when you delete the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@simonrwwhite.com.
