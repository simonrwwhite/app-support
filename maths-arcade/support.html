Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?

Go to Settings within the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

How do I change the game mode?

From the main menu, tap Play to see all available game modes including multiplication, division, addition, subtraction, and combination modes.

How does the level system work?

Each game mode has its own level. Completing a game successfully promotes you to the next level. Three consecutive failures at your current level will result in a demotion. Higher levels introduce harder questions.

How do I use voice input?

Voice input can be enabled in the app's Settings. You will need to grant microphone and speech recognition permissions when prompted. Speak your answer clearly and it will be recognised automatically.

What are the arcade games?

After completing the maths questions, you unlock an arcade game to play. Choose from Wildebeest Stampede, Banana Catch, or Monkey Mayhem.

Device Requirements
